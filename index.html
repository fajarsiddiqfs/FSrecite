Barakallahu Feekum.

Fajar Siddiq is a Singaporean Serial Entrepreneur. He is currently pursuing his hifz of the Qur’an and recites according to the riwayah of Ḥafṣ ‘an ‘Āṣim. Through his work in Qur’an media, da‘wah and technology, he records and produces video content featuring Qur’an reciters and imams, helping share their recitations and messages with a wider audience.

He is the founder of Euphorya and its brands, including IlmProduction.com, RemoteMuslims.com and RamadanMakers.com, and is also an ambassador for RawiApp.xyz. His initiatives focus on connecting, supporting and inspiring the Muslim community through digital media, technology and creative initiatives.

May Allah keep him steadfast upon His Book, strengthen his heart in faith, grant him sincerity in every step, and make the Qur’an the light of his heart, the guide of his life and a source of benefit to others. May Allah grant him strength and patience to complete his hifz, accept his efforts in da‘wah and service to the Ummah, and make his work a lasting source of khayr and sadaqah jariyah.

Allahumma Aameen.

    Barakallahu Feekum.

    Fajar Siddiq is a Singaporean Serial Entrepreneur. Born in the year 1986, currently based & living in Malaysia. He is currently pursuing his hifz of the Qur’an and recites according to the riwayah of Ḥafṣ ‘an ‘Āṣim. Through his work in Qur’an media, da‘wah and technology, he records and produces video content featuring Qur’an reciters and imams, helping share their recitations and messages with a wider audience.

    His connection with the Qur’an began through his parents. His late mother, who worked at the masjid, inspired in him a love for the masjid and the importance of serving the Muslim community. His father introduced him to QuranExplorer.com, his first experience of the Qur’an through a digital platform on desktop. Even after his father passed away, Fajar continued using it as a personal connection to the Qur’an and a lasting reminder of him.

    Fajar had already founded Euphorya in 2005, building his career as a self-taught entrepreneur, designer, developer and working remotely as a digital nomad. Following the passing of his mother, father and elder brother in 2012 and 2013, he embarked on an ‘Umrah that became a profound turning point in his life. During the journey, he had the opportunity to meet the Ex Grand Mufti of Makkah. After introducing himself and sharing what he did for a living, he was asked a simple yet profound question: “What have you done for Islam?”

    That question stayed with him. From that point, he began looking at the skills he had spent years developing not simply as a profession, but as an amanah that could also be used in service of Islam and the Ummah.

    He also had the chance to meet and record a recitation video with one of the Imams of Masjidil Haram, who shared:

    Just as we clean our homes when dust gathers, we need to clean our hearts when life, distractions and sins leave their traces. The Qur’an is one of the ways Allah brings light, remembrance and healing back into the heart.

    Watch Recitation Video: Sheikh Prof. Dr. Hassan Bukhari - Surah Ash-Shuraa (42:51–53)

    As the founder of Euphorya, he later began giving back to the community and building brands, including IlmProduction.com, RemoteMuslims.com and RamadanMakers.com, and is also an ambassador for Rawi App. His initiatives focus on connecting, supporting and inspiring the Muslim community through digital media, technology and creative initiatives.

    May Allah keep him steadfast upon His Book, strengthen his heart in faith, grant him sincerity in every step, and make the Qur’an the light of his heart, the guide of his life and a source of benefit to others. May Allah grant him strength and patience to complete his hifz, accept his efforts in da‘wah and service to the Ummah, and make his work a lasting source of khayr and sadaqah jariyah.

    PS: The purpose of this website is to document my Qur’an journey, build a lasting relationship with the Quran, stay productive through it, and leave a legacy of good deeds.

    Allahumma Aameen.
